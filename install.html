Tester guide - Playtest #3Thanks for helping test! Voxel Arena is a 5v5 MOBA in the style of Mobile Legends, played against bots (AI). It is still in development, so your feedback matters a lot.

What's new in #3

  • 52 heroes, all with new art.
  • 17 heroes have 4 skills or switch forms. Every hero has named combos (see hero select, the loading screen and Pause > Hero guide).
  • Mobile Legends-style shop: AUTO (on by default) buys your items for you. Some items have an active you can tap.
  • Bigger maps (165 x 165) with walled towns, and an easier jungle.
  • Epic spawn altars in each team's base.
  • Pick your lane before the match (Gold, EXP, Mid, Jungle, Roam), a guide arrow to your lane, and a bot jungler on your team.
  • Shoot while moving (no need to stop first).
  • Clear skill aiming and a clear marker on who your attacks are hitting.
  • Painted skill buttons.
  • Pings and quick chat. The bots answer your pings.
  • Replays: tap Watch replay after a match, or Profile > Replays.
  • New splash and loading screens.

Install and update

iPhone

  1. Open this page in Safari (not Chrome) and tap Play now.
  2. Tap Share (the square with the up arrow) > Add to Home Screen > Add.
  3. Start the game from the new icon and turn the phone sideways.

Already installed Playtest #2? Open the game from its icon while online. When New version - tap to reload shows, tap it. If it does not show: close the app (swipe it away), open it again and wait a moment.

Android

  1. Tap Download Android app above > open the file > Install. If Android asks, allow "Install unknown apps" for your browser once. It is a test build, so Play Protect may ask you to confirm.
  2. Already have the Playtest #2 app? Install the new APK over it. No need to uninstall.
  3. Or with Chrome: tap Play now > ⋮ menu > Install app (or Add to Home screen). To update: when New version - tap to reload shows, tap it.

After the first load, games vs bots work with no internet.

What to test

  1. Phone controls: are the joystick, attack and skill buttons easy to hit? Do your fingers cover the action?
  2. Attacking while moving: hold the attack button and keep walking. Do the shots land? Does it feel smooth?
  3. Each hero's combos: try many heroes, especially the ones with 4 skills or forms. Do the combos work as described?
  4. Shop and AUTO: with AUTO on, does it buy sensible items? Do the tap-to-use items work?
  5. Jungle: pick the Jungle lane. Are the camps easy to clear? Is the route easy to follow?
  6. Map size: too big, too small, or right? Is the walk to your lane too long?
  7. Match length: a match should take 15-30 minutes. How long did yours take?
  8. Lag and heat: does the game stutter? Does the phone get very hot? Does the battery drain fast?

How to report

Tell Eddie directly (chat or message) with:

  • Your phone model (for example iPhone 13, Samsung A54)
  • The map
  • The hero
  • What happened (and what you expected)
  • A screenshot or screen recording (helps a lot)

Known limits

  • Vs AI (bots) only.
  • Online play is coming soon.
  • Balance is still being tuned (some heroes may be too strong or too weak).
  • The Android app is a test build, not on the Play Store. iPhone uses Add to Home Screen.
